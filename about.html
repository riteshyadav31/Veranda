Property listings without the guesswork

Veranda started because finding a straight answer about a house is harder than it should be.

What we do

We list homes, plots and commercial space across Uttar Pradesh, with the details that actually decide a purchase: real built-up area, the floor, the facing direction, and who you will be speaking to.

Every listing is posted by the owner or by an agent we have verified. Photos are required. Prices are shown in full, with nothing hidden behind a phone call.

Verified before published

Direct contact
